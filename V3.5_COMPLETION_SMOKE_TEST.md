# V3.5 Accurate Proposed Load — Smoke Test

Run this checklist on the `v3.5-completion-pass` branch before merging to `main`.

1. Open `index.html` through Live Server. The port may be 5500, 5503, or another available local port; the port does not affect the test.
2. Confirm the existing Dashboard, Business Setup, Customer / New Load, Pickup, Delivery, Finance, and Drivers & Equipment views still open.
3. In Transportation Intelligence / V3.5 classification, save a fully verified driver/equipment classification with valid truck/trailer GVWR, empty weights, and GCWR.
4. In the V3.5 Load Decision form, confirm the new Complete Proposed-Load Economics panel appears.
5. Test blended MPG with loaded miles, pickup deadhead, return/repositioning deadhead, fuel price, offer, costs, and target profit.
6. Confirm Total Miles includes loaded + pickup deadhead + return/repositioning deadhead.
7. Confirm Walk-away equals the complete estimated cost and Recommended Ask uses the configured negotiation allowance.
8. Enter target margin, profit-per-mile floor, and hourly floor. Confirm the Target uses the highest configured requirement.
9. Enter an offer below walk-away and confirm PASS ON LOAD.
10. Enter an offer above walk-away but below target and confirm NEGOTIATE RATE plus a counteroffer.
11. Enter an offer meeting/exceeding target and confirm ACCEPT LOAD when dispatch checks pass.
12. Switch to Separate Loaded / Empty MPG and confirm both MPG inputs are required and the blended MPG field is disabled.
13. Fail a compliance/weight condition and confirm DO NOT DISPATCH remains enforced.
14. Submit two valid evaluations and confirm Estimate History increments rather than replacing the previous saved estimate.
15. Open V3.5 Test Gate and confirm the Blueprint-Aligned Gate passes after a complete saved estimate.
16. Confirm Drivers & Equipment trip lock still blocks unverified/expired driver or incomplete truck/trailer records and records the blocked attempt in the audit trail.

Do not merge to `main` unless all applicable checks pass without browser-console errors.
