# V3.7 Financial Close

## Release outcome

For one immutable Load ID, Family Legacy Commercial Command must reconcile proof of delivery, an itemized invoice, payments, receivable balance, double-entry ledger postings, and final profit. A second load must never influence any value in that reconciliation.

## Decisions carried forward

- Keep the workflow fast for a one-to-three-driver operation while preserving records that can scale.
- Enter data once and auto-populate it downstream; finance consumes the signed load, approved estimate, actual-trip expenses, receipts, vendors, and POD.
- Keep estimated values separate from actual and posted financial values.
- Preserve the expense categories Fuel, Driver pay, Tolls, Maintenance, and Other.
- Require confirmation before finalizing an invoice, posting an adjustment, reversing a payment, or closing a load.
- Posted records are append-and-reverse; they are never silently edited or deleted.
- Tax treatment is reporting metadata on financial entries; it does not silently reduce trip profit. Tax filing and advice remain outside this browser prototype.
- Document object storage and OCR remain V4.0 work. V3.7 uses existing receipt, signature, POD, and invoice metadata.

## Delivery milestones

### 1. Reconciliation foundation

- Compute invoice total, payments, receivable, expenses, earned profit, and balanced journal totals from the selected Load ID only.
- Display the reconciliation on Finance and add a V3.7 gate.
- Add a deterministic two-load isolation check.

### 2. Itemized invoice lifecycle

- Draft invoice from the signed load and POD.
- Show base transportation, each approved accessorial, credits/adjustments, and total due as distinct line items.
- Snapshot terms, due date, bill-to, Load ID, and POD reference at finalization.
- Require confirmation to finalize; finalized invoices are immutable except by credit or reversal.

### 3. Payments and receivables

- Give every payment a stable ID, date, method, reference, amount, and invoice allocation.
- Support open, partially paid, paid, and overpaid balances without hiding overpayments.
- Reverse incorrect payments with a linked compensating record.

### 4. Double-entry ledger

- Finalized invoice: debit Accounts Receivable; credit Transportation Revenue and Accessorial Revenue by line item.
- Customer payment: debit Cash/Undeposited Funds; credit Accounts Receivable.
- Expense: debit its expense category; credit Cash/Payables.
- Credits, adjustments, and reversals use linked balanced entries.

### 5. Financial close gate

The gate passes only when POD is complete, invoice lines equal invoice total, payments equal allocations, receivable equals invoice less payments and adjustments, debits equal credits, final profit reconciles, and the isolation test passes.

## Explicitly deferred

- Authentication, database persistence, tenant isolation, durable object storage, backups, and server audit logs (V4.0).
- Tax calculation or filing advice.
- Bank-feed reconciliation and processor settlement integrations.
